These marks identify compatible tools. They are owned by their respective
companies, are not covered by this repository's MIT license, and do not imply
endorsement or a partnership with AgentHangar.

Sources retrieved on 2026-10-02:

- **Claude:** [Anthropic press kit](https://www.anthropic.com/press-kit),
  `Claude logo - Slate.svg` and `Claude logo - Ivory.svg`, unchanged.
- **OpenAI:** wordmark SVG displayed in the header of the official
  [design guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/). The supplied path and viewBox
  are unchanged; presentation uses black or white in place of the website's
  inherited `currentColor`. OpenAI owns the OpenAI name and logo.
- **Cursor:** [official brand assets](https://cursor.com/brand),
  `LOCKUP_HORIZONTAL_2D_LIGHT.svg` and `LOCKUP_HORIZONTAL_2D_DARK.svg`, unchanged.

The `light` files are for light backgrounds; `dark` files are for dark backgrounds.
Keep proportions and clear space. Do not recolor, stretch, or add effects.
